The idea

An island with room to breathe.

Nivara Coast is a private island concept built around one quiet luxury: space. Villas stand apart along the lagoon and the shore, each facing its own stretch of water.

Inside, pale timber, white linen and natural stone keep every room calm, so the colour comes from the sea and the sky.

  • White sand

    A soft white beach follows the shoreline, shaded by palms.

  • Clear water

    A sheltered turquoise lagoon, calm and shallow, runs out to the reef.

  • Open rooms

    Glass doors slide away, so each room opens onto its deck and the view.

The Villas

Three ways to stay

Over the lagoon or on the sand. Every villa stands apart, with its own deck and its own view of the water.

The Island

The island at a glance

A ring of white sand, a calm turquoise lagoon and a reef beyond it. Villas follow the boardwalk out over the water or sit back among the palms.

The Lagoon

Where the pool ends, the lagoon begins.

The infinity edge lines up with the horizon, so the pool seems to run straight out to sea.

Dining

Long lunches above the water

The terrace stands over the lagoon, open to the breeze on every side. Rattan chairs, white linen and ceiling fans turning slowly overhead.

Come for breakfast in the cool of the morning and stay as long as the light allows.

Arrival

Arrive slowly.

Walk out along the boardwalk as the light turns gold, with clear water on either side and your villa waiting at the end.
