Privacy policy

MONO is a demo shop with no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. This page says exactly what it keeps, where it keeps it, and how to clear it.

Last updated 14 October 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party script.
  • Forms send nothing. Messages and newsletter sign-ups go nowhere.
  • Your bag, demo orders and admin edits are saved only in this browser.
  • You can clear all of it at any time.

What is kept in your browser

The demo uses your browser's own storage so the bag, checkout and admin keep working as you move around. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to MONO or to anyone else, and other visitors cannot see it. The site's own code sets no cookies.

mono.cart.v1Local storage
HoldsThe products, options and quantities in your bag.
WhySo your bag is still there on the next page and after a reload.
mono.orders.v1Local storage
HoldsDemo orders placed at the checkout, including the name, email, phone number and delivery address you typed.
WhySo the order confirmation and the demo admin can show your orders.
mono.catalog.v1Local storage
HoldsProducts you added or changed in the demo admin, and stock used up by demo orders.
WhySo your edits show across the shop in this browser.
mono.checkout.draft.v1Session storage
HoldsWhat you have typed into the checkout form so far.
WhySo Back or a reload does not empty the form. It is removed when you place the order or close the tab.
mono:vt-fromSession storage
HoldsThe address of the page you came from.
WhyTo animate the product image between pages. It is removed when you close the tab.

What is not collected

There are no accounts, so there are no passwords or profiles. There are no analytics tools, tracking pixels or advertising tags, and the fonts and images come from this site rather than from other services.

The checkout has no card fields and takes no payment, so no card details are ever asked for or kept.

Forms

The contact form, the newsletter box and the checkout all run inside your browser. Submitting them sends nothing over the network. The checkout saves the order you place in this browser, as described above, so you can see it again. The other forms keep nothing at all.

The host

MONO is a set of static files on a web host. Like most hosts, it may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, the pages requested and browser details, to keep the service secure and working. The demo itself does nothing with them.

Clearing the data

Use the reset button in How this demo works to remove the bag, demo orders and admin edits from this browser in one step. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings. A private window clears everything when you close it.

Changes to this policy

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. MONO is a fictional label with no inbox, so there is no one to write to about it; the contact page explains more.
