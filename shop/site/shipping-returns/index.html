Shipping and returns

Questions

Good to know

Short answers about delivery, payment and returns in this demo.

Will my order arrive?

No. MONO is a fictional shop made as a portfolio demo. Orders are saved in your browser so you can see the confirmation, but nothing is packed or sent.

Am I charged anything?

No. The checkout has no card fields and takes no payment. The test card and cash on delivery options are both part of the demo.

How is the delivery price worked out?

Standard delivery is $6, or free when your bag reaches $150. Express delivery is $14 on any order. The checkout shows the full total before you place the order.

Where do you deliver?

Nowhere, as nothing ships. The checkout accepts any address so you can try the whole flow.

Can I track my order?

There is no tracking. Your demo orders appear on the order confirmation page and in the demo admin, in this browser only.

How do I start a return?

There is nothing to return in this demo. The returns policy above shows how returns would work in a real shop.
