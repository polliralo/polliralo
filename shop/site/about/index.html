The label

About MONO

MONO is a fictional menswear label, made as a portfolio demo. The idea behind it is simple: fewer, better essentials, chosen to work together.

The idea

Fewer, better essentials

MONO starts from a short list: the shirts, tees and polos a man reaches for most, and the few accessories that finish them. Nothing seasonal, nothing loud.

The colours are chosen to sit together: ivory, charcoal, olive, navy, black and brown. The intent is that any top in the range works with any watch, belt or pair of sunglasses, so getting dressed takes less thought.

  1. 01

    Fewer pieces

    Twelve pieces in six categories. A short range is easier to choose from and easier to combine.

  2. 02

    Quiet colours

    Muted, natural shades, so a new piece joins the rest of the wardrobe instead of fighting it.

  3. 03

    Plain words

    Each product page says what the piece is, shows it from more than one side and suggests what to wear it with.

Demo

How this demo works

MONO looks and behaves like a shop, so you can try every step. None of it is real, and nothing you do here is shared.

Prices and stock
Every price and stock number is demo data, set for illustration only.
Saved in this browser
Your bag, demo orders and any admin edits are saved only in this browser (localStorage). They never leave your device, and other visitors cannot see them.
Checkout
The checkout takes no payment and has no card fields, so it collects no card details. Placing an order only saves a demo record in this browser.
Store admin
The demo admin has no sign-in and is not secure. Anyone can open it, and changes only affect the browser they were made in.
Forms
The contact and newsletter forms send nothing. See the privacy policy for the full list of what is kept.

Start again

Clear the bag, demo orders and admin edits from this browser. The shop goes back to its original demo data.
