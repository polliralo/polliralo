Measuring

How to measure

Use a soft tape measure and stand relaxed. Keep the tape level, snug but not tight.

  1. 01

    Chest

    Under the arms, around the fullest part of the chest.

  2. 02

    Neck

    Around the base of the neck, with one finger under the tape.

  3. 03

    Waist

    Around your natural waist, just above the hip bones.

Between two sizes? Choose the larger size for an easier fit, or the smaller one for a closer fit.
