## Fact-check of the MONO technical SEO checklist (2026-10-14)

Most of the checklist is right. I found two corrections and two smaller fixes; all of them affect only the live-mode markup. Demo mode is unaffected.

**How I checked:** developers.google.com, its .cn mirror, schema.org and developers.cloudflare.com were all blocked by the proxy here as well. For Google I relied on search extracts of the official pages and changelogs. For Cloudflare, schema.org and Lighthouse I read the primary sources directly from their GitHub repos (cloudflare-docs, schemaorg `data/schema.ttl`, GoogleChrome/lighthouse `main`).

### Wrong or needs changing

1. **§8a: the multi-page ProductGroup puts too much on each colour page.**
   - **The problem:** the example fully defines the navy variants, with price and availability, on the olive page.
   - **What Google's pattern is:** Google's multi-page pattern says the full ProductGroup is repeated on each variant page, but it "has a full definition of the variants on the page as well as a variant with only the url property to link to the variants on the other page".
   - **The fix:** on `/products/olive-pique-polo/`, `hasVariant` = full `Product` entries for the 5 olive sizes, plus `{ "url": "https://…/products/navy-pique-polo/?size=S" }` stubs for the 5 navy sizes. Do the reverse on the navy page.
   - **Why it matters:** this also satisfies the checklist's own rule that markup must match visible content. For example, navy XXL being `OutOfStock` is not shown on the olive page.
   - **Still correct:** no `url` on the ProductGroup (Google: there is no single URL for the group in the multi-page setup).
   - Source: [Google, Product variant structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-variants).

2. **§7: offer-level `OfferShippingDetails` does not require `deliveryTime`.**
   - **Required:** `shippingDestination` (with `addressCountry`) and `shippingRate`, which needs `currency` plus `value` or `maxValue`, and only one rate per `OfferShippingDetails`.
   - **Recommended, not required:** `deliveryTime` (`handlingTime`/`transitTime`). The Rich Results Test reports a missing `deliveryTime` only as a recommended-property warning.
   - **Impact:** low, because the plan uses Organization-level shipping. Fix the wording anyway so a test doesn't fail on this.
   - Sources: [Google, merchant listing](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-now-supports-shipping-details-schema-for-product-results-340924).

3. **§8c: put the shipping policy markup on the shipping policy page, not the home page.**
   - **What Google says:** for the site-wide `Organization` → `hasShippingService` policy, Google recommends placing the markup on the page that describes the shipping policy. It also does not need to be on every page.
   - **The fix:** in live mode, emit an `Organization`/`OnlineStore` node with the same `@id` (`…/#org`) on `/shipping-returns/`, carrying `hasShippingService` and `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`. Keep the home page block to WebSite + basic Organization.
   - **Confidence:** medium. This comes from search extracts and coverage of Google's text, not the page itself.
   - Sources: [Google, shipping policy (ShippingService)](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/shipping-policy); [Google Search Central blog, 12 Nov 2025](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/11/more-ways-to-share-shipping); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-launches-structured-data-for-merchant-shipping-policies/560628/).

4. **§7: date fix.** The checklist says "Since Nov 2025, Google recommends one site-wide policy under Organization … and `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`", but the two parts started at different times:
   - **Organization-level return policy markup:** available since 2024.
   - **12 Nov 2025:** Google added Organization-level shipping (`ShippingService`) and opened the Search Console "Shipping and returns" settings to all sites it identifies as merchants.
   - **No change needed:** the rest of that section holds. Search Console settings override the markup, and product-level markup overrides the Organization-level policy.
   - Source: [Google Search Central blog, Nov 2025](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/11/more-ways-to-share-shipping).

### Checked and correct (no change needed)

**Cloudflare Workers static assets**, read from the primary docs:
- `not_found_handling` defaults to `"none"`. `"404-page"` returns a 404 with the nearest `404.html`. `"single-page-application"` returns 200 with `index.html`.
- `html_handling` defaults to `"auto-trailing-slash"`, so `foo/index.html` is served at `foo/`.
- `_headers` allows up to 100 rules, with 2,000 characters per line. It is not applied to responses generated by Worker code.
- Sources: [headers.mdx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-docs/production/src/content/docs/workers/static-assets/headers.mdx); [wrangler configuration.mdx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-docs/production/src/content/docs/workers/wrangler/configuration.mdx); [static-site-generation.mdx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-docs/production/src/content/docs/workers/static-assets/routing/static-site-generation.mdx).

**§8c `ShippingService` nesting (one of the two points the checklist flagged)** is valid schema.org:
- `Organization.hasShippingService` → `ShippingService`.
- `ShippingService.handlingTime` takes a `ServicePeriod`.
- `ShippingService.shippingConditions` → `ShippingConditions`, which takes `shippingDestination`, `shippingRate` (`MonetaryAmount`) and `transitTime` (`ServicePeriod`).
- `ServicePeriod.duration` takes a `QuantitativeValue`.
- Source: [schema.org schema.ttl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/schemaorg/schemaorg/main/data/schema.ttl).
- **`audience` (the other flagged point):** schema.org allows `Product.audience` and `PeopleAudience.suggestedGender`. I could not open Google's page to confirm its mapping of `suggestedMinAge: 13` to "adult", which matches its published age-group table as I know it, so keep the planned Rich Results Test check for this.

**Product markup changes on Google's side:**
- `category` (Text or `CategoryCode`) and the "Sale duration" section (`validFrom` / `validThrough` / `priceValidUntil`, on the Offer or the PriceSpecification) were added on 7 July 2026. Sources: [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-merchant-listings-support-sale-duration-and-product-category-481730); [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-product-category-sale-duration-merchant-listing-41637.html).
- `hasAdultConsideration` was added as a recommended property on 20 May 2026. Source: [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-adds-adult-consideration-41355.html).
- `returnPolicyCountry` is required for `MerchantReturnPolicy` (changelog, 14 March 2025). The `applicableCountry` maximum is 50 (raised from 25 in April 2023). Source: [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-updates-structured-data-requirements-for-return-policies/542080/).

**Search features:**
- The sitelinks search box was retired on 21 Nov 2024; WebSite site-name markup is still supported.
- Mobile results have shown only the domain, without breadcrumbs, since 23 Jan 2025; desktop still shows breadcrumbs. Source: [Google blog](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/01/simplifying-breadcrumbs).
- AVIF has been supported in Google Search since August 2024. Source: [Google blog, "Happy AVIFriday"](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/08/happy-avifriday).

**Crawling docs:** the faceted-navigation page moved to developers.google.com/crawling on 18 Dec 2025 (second migration batch; the first was 20 Nov 2025). Sources: [Google crawling changelog](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/changelog); [faceted navigation page](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation).

**Policy and penalties:**
- Spam reports may lead to manual action (documentation changed 14 Apr 2026). Small update: since about 24 Apr 2026, a report that contains personal information is not processed. Sources: [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/google-spam-reports-manual-actions-policy-update); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-reverses-spam-report-policy-no-pii-submissions-processed/).
- A structured-data manual action removes rich-result eligibility but "doesn't affect how the page ranks". The guideline "Don't use structured data to deceive or mislead users" exists as quoted. Source: [Google, structured data guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies).

**Lighthouse:** the SEO category still includes `is-crawlable` ("Page is blocked from indexing") on the current `main` branch. Source: [Lighthouse default-config.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GoogleChrome/lighthouse/main/core/config/default-config.js).

**Long-standing facts that still hold** (not re-checked against a primary page):
- Core Web Vitals "good" thresholds: LCP 2.5 s, INP 200 ms, CLS 0.1, with poor at 4 s / 500 ms / 0.25. CrUX uses a 28-day window.
- Sitemaps: Google ignores `priority` and `changefreq`, and uses `lastmod` only when it is consistently accurate. In image sitemaps only `image:loc` is used.
- Product requirements:
  - product snippet = `name` plus one of offers/review/aggregateRating;
  - merchant listing = name, image, offers, price;
  - `priceCurrency` is "required for merchant listing experiences".
- Organization and breadcrumbs:
  - the Organization logo must be at least 112×112 px;
  - the last breadcrumb `item` may be omitted;
  - images should be at least 50K pixels in 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9;
  - Google warns that JavaScript-generated Product markup makes Shopping crawls less reliable.
- Titles: the "more than 80%" figure is consistent with Google's 2021 statement (about 87%).
